# Policies and support

These are the rules specific to SLOP6608. The university's own rules still
apply; nothing below replaces them. What follows is the part a general policy
cannot cover, because it concerns a text that is easier to paraphrase than to
find.

## What counts as a citation

A citation names the 卷, and for the five 山經 chapters the 次 section and the
mountain or water the record is filed under. It quotes the line character for
character, in the traditional forms the edition prints. The edition this course
reads is the 四庫全書 text of the 山海經 (Shānhǎijīng) with 郭璞's commentary,
as transcribed at Wikisource. Any complete edition is acceptable if you name
the one you used.

Transliterating a quotation into simplified characters is altering it. The
course's own prose is simplified; quoted lines are not. So 肥遗 (féiyí) in a
sentence and 肥遺 inside a quoted line are both correct, in their places.

## When you cannot find the line

Mark the entry `verified: false`, say where you looked, and stop.

This is not penalised. It is the correct result, and in several cases the only
honest one. An unverified entry claims nothing after that point: no omen, no
use, no confident prose around the gap. Marked that way it is a complete
submission and can score full marks.

What is penalised is filling the gap. Supplying a plausible line, reconstructing
one from a translation, or paraphrasing into quotation marks is fabrication of
evidence, and it is treated as misconduct rather than as a citation error. The
rule applies to staff exactly as it applies to students: two of this course's
weekly lines were wrong until the edition was opened, and the corrections are
recorded rather than quietly made.

## Translations and the edition

Read translations. Birrell, Strassberg and the annotated Chinese editions are
all useful, and a translation may sit beside a quotation in your work.

A translation may not stand in place of one. The disagreements this course is
built on — a tail that changes between two records, a name that arrives without
a body — are exactly the details a translator has to resolve in order to produce
readable English. The resolution is the translator's argument, not the text.

## AI assistance

Use of an assistant is permitted and must be declared, in a short note naming
the tool and what it did.

The declaration is not the check. The check is that you can locate any line you
cited, in the edition, while someone watches. Assistants generate *Classic of Mountains and Seas*
quotations fluently and incorrectly, and a fabricated line is the one failure
this course is guaranteed to find, because every citation in every submission is
looked up. A line that cannot be found is unverified and unpenalised, provided
you declared it so before the marker found it.

Assistance with English prose, structure and formatting is unrestricted, and
still declared.

## Late work

Five percent of the available marks per day, to five days, after which the work
is not marked. Extensions are granted for the usual reasons, through the usual
form, and asking early costs nothing.

The specimen cards are the exception worth stating. They are a running
submission written across twelve weeks, and a late hand-in of the set attracts
the ordinary penalty. Cards written in the last fortnight are not late and are
not a misconduct matter. They simply do not work, because twelve cards written
at once all sort by the same rule.

## Being wrong

A well-argued reclassification the marker disagrees with scores above an
unargued one the marker shares.

This is a policy and not encouragement. The course's answer to its own question
is that the system can be built partly, and that the failures carry more of the
argument than the successes. Work is marked on the argument, the evidence and
the declared cost of holding a position, not on arriving at the reading the
convenor happens to hold. For 肥遗 and for 窫窳 (yàyǔ) there is no such reading.

## Getting help

Ask in the session, or by email to the convenor. Questions about whether a line
is really in the edition are always worth asking and never count against you.
