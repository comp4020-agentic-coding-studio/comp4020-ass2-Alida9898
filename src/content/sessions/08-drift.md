Here is the whole record at 青丘之山:

> 有獸焉，其狀如狐而九尾，其音如嬰兒，能食人；食者不蠱。

Count the names in it. There is one, and it is 青丘之山, the mountain. The beast
has none. It is a beast shaped like a fox, with nine tails, an infant's voice,
an appetite for people, and a use.

九尾狐 (jiǔwěihú) is not in the text. The bestiary prints the name because a
reader needs a handle, and the entry says so; the edition does not. This is the
single most consequential fact in the course, and it costs one line of reading
to confirm.

## What the record supplies, and what it cannot

Nine tails, fox shape, infant cry, man-eating, and 食者不蠱 — eat it and you
cannot be bewitched. That is the entry. Note what the last clause does: the
creature is the cure for bewitchment, not its cause.

Everything else attached to that name today arrived from somewhere else. The
fox that takes human form, the fox that seduces, the fox whose tails are a
count of centuries — none of it is in 南山經, and none of it contradicts 南山經
either, because the record is too short to contradict. Absence is what lets a
name grow.

## Drift with a paper trail

The session traces one name forward through 干宝's 搜神记 and 蒲松龄's 聊斋志异.
The point is not that the later books are wrong. They are not making taxonomic
claims. The point is that a system whose species are named by later readers has
imported later readers' conclusions as data.

狌狌 (xīngxīng) shows the same process happening inside the edition itself. It
is a 禺-like beast with white ears in 南山經, a pig-shaped one with a human face
in 海內南經, and in 海內經 it appears once more under a different spelling:

> 有青獸，人面，名曰猩猩。

One name, two graphs, three bodies, and the surviving modern word 猩猩 now
means an ape in a zoo. 讙头 (huāntóu) drifts in the other direction: a country
in one record and a creature in the other, spelled 讙頭 and then 驩頭.

精卫 (jīngwèi) is the counter-case, and it is why this week is not simply a
complaint. Its record already contains the story — the drowned daughter, the
stones carried to the eastern sea. The drift there is not a new body but a new
use, from an account of a bird to a figure for persistence. The record held the
material. Later readers chose which part of it to carry.

## Two hours, worked backwards

- **Thirty minutes.** Each of you writes down what you already believed about
  九尾狐 before opening the edition. This is collected.
- **Fifty minutes.** Reading the four later passages supplied, in order, and
  marking the first appearance of each property on the board.
- **Forty minutes.** Back to your own week 7 proposal. Find every species name
  in it that the edition does not print, and mark them.

## Afterwards

Your specimen card for this week carries a field the earlier cards did not: the
name's source. Where the edition names the creature, cite the line. Where it
does not, say who did.
