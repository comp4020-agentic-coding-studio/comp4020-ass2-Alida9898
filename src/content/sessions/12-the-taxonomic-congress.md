The rule has two clauses and the second one is the whole course. Citing a line
is easy and everyone will manage it. Declaring which of your own fields that
line does not supply means writing down, in your own submission, the list of
places where you decided rather than read.

Nothing else is grounds for rejection today. An entry may be eccentric,
badly-argued or wrong. It is admissible if it is honest about its gaps.

## Nine tails, three times

Consider what the rule does to a single trait.

> 有獸焉，其狀如羊，九尾四耳，其目在背，其名曰猼訑，佩之不畏。

猼訑 (bóshī) is a sheep with nine tails, four ears and eyes in its back. 陆吾
(lùwú) has nine tails on a tiger's body at 昆侖. The unnamed beast at 青丘之山
has nine tails on a fox. Three nine-tailed creatures, in three chapters, with
nothing else in common and no line connecting them.

An entry that treats *nine tails* as a taxonomic character is making a claim no
record makes. That entry is still admissible — but only if it says so, in the
declarations, in one sentence. An entry that quietly groups the three has
smuggled a conclusion into a data field, and the congress rejects it.

## Two records that will not resolve

窫窳 (yàyǔ) is the hard test and it is on the table deliberately. 海內南經 gives
it a dragon's head and a 貙's body, in the 弱水, eating people. 海內西經 gives
it a snake's body, a human face, and a death. Week 9 established that these
cannot be one animal.

So an entry on 窫窳 must pick a body and then declare that the pick is
unjustified, because nothing in either record ranks them. That is an
uncomfortable sentence to write about your own work. Writing it is the
assessment.

## Order and coincidence

Two smaller cases, because they catch different fields.

蛊雕 (gǔdiāo) is named before it is described. In its record 名曰蠱雕 arrives
ahead of 其狀如雕而有角, reversing the order every entry form in this course
assumes. An entry using this record has to declare that it reordered it. It also
has to decide whether the infant's cry it shares with the 青丘 beast is a
character or a formula, and declare which.

顒 (yú) catches the omen field. 見則天下大旱 appears on it word for word and on
at least five unrelated creatures. Any entry that fills an omen field is
filling a field week 5 showed belongs to the formula rather than to the animal,
and the declaration has to say so.

## How the congress runs

Two hours, procedural.

- **Fifteen minutes.** Entries tabled. Each is read aloud by its author, in
  ninety seconds, ending with the declarations.
- **Sixty minutes.** Challenges. One ground only: an undeclared field. The
  challenger names the field and the line that fails to supply it. The author
  either concedes and amends on the spot or shows the line.
- **Forty-five minutes.** The question the course opened with, answered in
  writing, individually, and read out. Can a rigorous classification be built
  for these creatures.

## Marks

The bestiary entry is worth 30 per cent and is due at the end of this week. The
peer reviews are worth 10 per cent, and a review is marked on whether it found
an undeclared field, not on its tone. A review that finds none and explains why
it looked in the right places is a full-marks review.

The answer this course argues for is *partly*. The successes are the fields
that held. The failures are the fields that did not, and they are the part
worth submitting.
