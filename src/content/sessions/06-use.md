Every field examined so far describes the creature. This one describes a
transaction. The rule is that use is the only field ranking creatures against
each other, and that a system sorted on it is sorting people's advantage rather
than kinds of animal.

## Four verbs

The slot has a grammar: a verb applied to the creature, then what the person
gets. 食之, eat it. 佩之, wear it. 服之, take it as medicine. 乘之, ride it. Four
relations, and the verb is stated more consistently than any part of the body.

鹿蜀 (lùshǔ) carries 佩之宜子孫, worn for the prospering of descendants. 猼訑
(bóshī) carries 佩之不畏, worn against fear. 旋龟 (xuánguī) carries 佩之不聾，可
以為底, worn against deafness and against calluses. Three worn creatures: a
horse-like beast, a sheep-like beast, and a bird-headed turtle. Sorted by use
they are one class. Sorted by body they are three, in three chapters. That is
the rule, demonstrated in one line, and the rest of the session is about whether
the class is worth having.

## What a person gets

九尾狐 (jiǔwěihú) states the benefit and the hazard in adjacent clauses: 能食人
and 食者不蠱. It eats people, and eating it protects against bewitchment. The
transaction runs both directions and the record does not mark either direction
as the more important fact.

肥遗 (féiyí) splits under its own name. The bird is filed under 英山:

> 有鳥焉，其狀如鶉，黃身而赤喙，其名曰肥遺，食之已癘，可以殺蟲。

The snake under the same name carries no use at all, only an omen. If use is the
sorting key then 肥遗 sorts in two places at once, which is week 9's problem
arriving three weeks early and worth noting in the margin now.

驺吾 (zōuwú) is the limit of the field:

> 林氏國有珍獸，大若虎，五采畢具，尾長于身，名曰騶吾，乘之日行千里。

珍獸, a treasure-beast. The most specific classification this record supplies is
a valuation, and the use clause is what justifies it. Of all the entries on the
course list, this is the one whose kind is a price.

## The blanks, and what a blank means here

蛊雕 (gǔdiāo) has no use. 帝江 (dìjiāng) has none. 旋龟's second record drops the
use its first record carried, so whether that creature has one depends on which
record was read. A ranking by benefit files all of those as unranked, and unranked is not
last place. It is a missing measurement, and the failure to notice the
difference is the most common one in submitted cards.

## How the two hours run

- **Twenty-five minutes.** The four verbs, and the benefits grouped by what they
  protect against.
- **Fifty-five minutes.** The by-use taxonomy built in groups across the whole
  course bestiary. Each group reports which bodies its sort puts together and
  names the pair it likes least.
- **Forty minutes.** The midterm brief, read together and questioned.
