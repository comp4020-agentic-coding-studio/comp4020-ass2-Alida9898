The system now has a rank above species, and it did not invent it. 神, 獸, 鳥
and 人 are the 山海经's (Shānhǎijīng) own classifiers, printed in the records
themselves, and taking them over is the cheapest move available. It is also the
least examined one. A borrowed rank carries whatever the lender was sorting by.
This week tests the assumption that what it was sorting by is the body.

## One mountain, two files

昆侖 has two guards. The first is introduced as an office:

> 神陸吾司之。其神狀虎身而九尾，人面而虎爪；是神也，司天之九部及帝之囿時。

The second stands on the same mountain, facing east:

> 開明獸身大類虎而九首，皆人面，東嚮立昆侖上。

陆吾 (lùwú) is a tiger's body with nine tails and a human face. 开明兽
(kāimíngshòu) is largely a tiger's body with nine heads, every one of them
human-faced. Both are nine-fold. Both are tiger below and human above. The
anatomy differs in one part, and a taxonomy that sorted by body would put them
in adjacent cells.

The text puts them in different kingdoms. One is a 神 and the other is a 獸,
and the difference between them in the record is not a limb. It is a job.

## 神 as an office

Read the first line again for its verb. 司之 — it administers. What follows the
description is a jurisdiction: the nine regions of heaven, and the seasons of
the god's park. The body is the middle clause, not the point.

烛阴 (zhúyīn) is the same shape of claim made larger. It is the 神 of 鍾山, and
what the record specifies is not anatomy but consequence: eyes open are day,
eyes closed are night, breath out is winter and in is summer. 帝江 (dìjiāng)
is filed as a 神 too, at 天山, and holds no office at all. It is a sack the
colour of cinnabar fire with six feet, four wings and no face, and the only
thing it is said to do is know song and dance.

Three 神, three unrelated reasons to be one. The word is not a rank. It is a
list of things the text declined to call animals.

## Where no kind-word appears

开明兽 carries its classifier inside its name and not in the grammar of its
record. 精卫 (jīngwèi) is given as a 鳥 — crow-shaped, patterned head, white
bill, red feet — and then the same sentence turns into the story of a drowned
girl who became it. The record holds a diagnosis and a biography. The entry
form has a slot for one.

Other records name nothing. Neither of the two passages behind 窫窳 (yàyǔ)
says what kind of thing it is. Every proposal submitted this week has a kind
field, so every proposal has to say what that field does when the source is
silent: leave it empty, infer it from the body, or refuse the entry until
someone argues for one.

## The midterm dispute

Two hours, and the middle hour is adversarial.

- **Twenty-five minutes.** The four kind-words, counted rather than discussed.
  Where each appears in the formula, and how often it is missing.
- **Fifty minutes.** The dispute. One side argues that 开明兽 is a misfiled 神;
  the other that 陆吾 is a 獸 with a job. Later commentary is not admissible.
  Lines are.
- **Forty-five minutes.** Proposals on the table. Yours is read by someone who
  did not write it, and asked one question: what does your kind field do with
  开明兽.

## What you leave with

A ruling you have defended out loud, and a note of the objection you could not
answer. The system proposal is due at the end of this week and is worth 25 per
cent. It is marked on whether its rules are stated tightly enough to be broken,
which is what weeks 9 to 12 will do to them.
