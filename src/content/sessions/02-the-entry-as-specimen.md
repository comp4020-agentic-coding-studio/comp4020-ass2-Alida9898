The unit of this course is not the creature. It is the record: one sentence, or
two, filed under a mountain. This week's rule is that the record has a fixed
shape, and that the shape's slots are the first three taxonomic characters
available to us.

The abstraction is ours, not the text's. X is whatever the creature is compared
to. Y is the part distinguishing it from X. Z is the consequence of seeing it.
Around those three sit a name, sometimes a voice, sometimes a use. The edition
prints no such pattern. The pattern is what remains when the records are
stacked.

One correction before the columns are ruled. The middle slot is written 首 here
because heads are the commonest thing counted, but the slot takes any counted
part: 四角, 九尾, 四耳. The slot is the count, not the head.

## Slot by slot

鹿蜀 (lùshǔ) is the cleanest instance in the book. Comparison, parts, voice,
name, use, in that order and nothing else. Every slot filled, no residue.

夫诸 (fūzhū) is the shortest complete record here:

> 有獸焉，其狀如白鹿而四角，名曰夫諸，見則其邑大水。

Comparison, one counted part, name, omen. Four clauses. No voice, no use, no
size, no number of individuals. Everything a taxonomy would ask for next is
absent, and the absence is not marked as an absence.

軨軨 (línglíng) adds both optional slots: a voice, and the note that it calls its
own name. Self-naming recurs often enough to deserve a column of its own, and it
is the only slot reporting what a creature does rather than what it resembles.

## Where the order breaks

蛊雕 (gǔdiāo), filed under 鹿吳之山, runs the formula backwards:

> 水有獸焉，名曰蠱雕，其狀如雕而有角，其音如嬰兒之音，是食人。

Named first, described second. The slots are all present; the sequence is not.
If the shape is a rule of composition, 蛊雕 breaks it. If it is a tendency, then
this week's rule is weaker than its wording, and the parts grammar of week 3
has to carry more of the system than planned.

Two further pressures, both to be written down rather than resolved. 蛊雕's voice
is an infant's, which is also the voice of the nine-tailed beast at 青丘之山:
one slot value shared by two creatures with nothing else in common. And Z is
missing from two of these four records, so the third character is optional. A
character a specimen may simply not have is a column that fills with blanks.

## Two hours

- **Thirty minutes.** The formula on the board, the four records underneath it,
  the slots ruled as columns.
- **Fifty minutes.** In pairs, fill the columns for five further records drawn
  from 南山經 and 中山經. Each pair reports one record that would not fit.
- **Forty minutes.** The specimen card format is fixed by vote. It is the format
  every card uses this semester, including the final entry in week 12, so a
  column added now is a column filled twelve times.

## Hand in

The first specimen card that counts is due at the end of this week: one
creature, every slot, blanks left visibly blank. A card that fills a blank with
an inference and does not say it inferred it is the one failure marked hard.
