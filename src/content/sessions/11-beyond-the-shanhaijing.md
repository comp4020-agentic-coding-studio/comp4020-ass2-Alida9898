Every reconstruction is an argument about a record, made in a medium that
cannot show its working. A model has a size whether or not the record gives
one. A rendered creature has a skin, a gait and a weight. None of those are
optional in a game and none of them are in the text.

This week audits that. The method depends on one accident: these records are
short. A reconstruction of a nineteenth-century field description is impossible
to audit, because the description already specifies more than any viewer can
hold. Three clauses can be held in view, and against three clauses every
addition is visible.

## Three clauses

> 有獸焉，其狀如白鹿而四角，名曰夫諸，見則其邑大水。

That is the complete record of 夫诸 (fūzhū). A white deer, four horns, and a
town flooded. Now list what a model needs and the record does not supply: how
large, where the four horns sit and whether they branch, what colour its eyes
are, how it holds its head, whether it walks or stands, whether the water is
caused by it or merely announced by it. Six decisions before anybody has drawn
a hoof, and the record settles none of them.

The last of those is not a visual question and it is the one that changes the
creature most. 見則其邑大水 is a conditional. A reconstruction that shows the
beast summoning a flood has converted an omen into an agent, which is the same
move week 5 refused when it kept omens out of the character set.

## Where comparison runs out

夔 (kuí) has one foot. The record does not say how it moves, and a model has to
move. 出入水則必風雨 says it goes in and out of water; whether that is a wade,
a dive or a stride is supplied by whoever animates it.

帝江 (dìjiāng) is harder, and it is why this week ends on it. Every other
composite in the bestiary is assembled from animals a reader knows — a tiger's
body, a crane's leg, a snake's tail. 帝江 is assembled from none. It is a
yellow sack, red as cinnabar fire, six feet, four wings, and 渾敦無面目 — a
blur, no face. A renderer has to draw an absence, and every way of drawing an
absence is a positive choice: a smooth surface, a shadow, a smear, a mask.
Then, because the record says 是識歌舞, the thing has to dance.

## When the audit stops working

九尾狐 (jiǔwěihú) is the case where the method fails, and the failure is
instructive. Its reconstructions are no longer being made from the record. They
are being made from earlier reconstructions, which were made from the tradition
week 8 traced, which was made from a line that does not even name it. The
additions are no longer legible because nobody involved is looking at the
source.

## The workshop

Two hours, in two halves.

- **Fifty minutes.** Reconstructions are supplied, with their records printed
  beside them. Working in pairs, you annotate the image with every decision the
  record does not make, and mark each one as consistent, inconsistent, or
  unresolvable.
- **Seventy minutes.** You make one. Any medium, including a sketch on paper,
  of any creature in the bestiary, accompanied by the list of your own
  additions. The list is the deliverable. The picture is evidence for it.
