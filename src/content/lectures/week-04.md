The last three weeks took characters from the body. This one takes a character
from outside it, and finds that the field it goes in is two fields wearing one
name.

## Outline

- How a 山經 route is stated: direction, distance, mountain, contents
- What such a chain supports, which is a drawing, and what a drawing supports,
  which is disagreement
- How a 海經 position is stated: 在其南, 在海中, and a sequence with no declared
  origin
- Countries as loci, and the entries where a people and a creature are the same
  record
- The schema consequence, in one line: one column or two

## Why one column is two

A mappable position and a sequence position are not two grades of the same
information. They are different assertions. A bearing says where a thing is
relative to the world; a sequence says where a record is relative to the record
before it. The first can be checked against terrain. The second can only be
checked against the text, which is to say against itself.

A taxonomy that puts both in one column produces cards that look uniform and are
not, and uniform-looking cards are what a marker and a classifier both read
fastest.

## What this does not claim

The 海經 positions are not vaguer versions of 山經 positions, and they are not
mistakes. They are precise about a different thing. 烛阴 (zhúyīn) lies below 鍾山
in the outer north with a body a thousand 里 long, and the length is stated
more exactly than any mountain's height in the whole text.

## What to bring

Bring paper and a ruler. The session's first half is a drawing exercise and the
drawing has to be yours, because the sentence where it fails is only convincing
to the person who was drawing it.
